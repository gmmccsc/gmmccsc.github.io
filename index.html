GOVT. M.M. CITY COLLEGE

Govt. M.M. City College, one of the oldest and most prestigious institutions in khulna, has a rich legacy of academic excellence and holistic development. Established in 1965, the college has consistently produced top scholars, leaders, and professionals who have made significant contributions to society. With a dedicated faculty, state-of-the-art facilities, and a strong emphasis on both academics and extracurricular activities, Govt. M.M. City College fosters an environment where students thrive. Its commitment to nurturing critical thinking, discipline, and creativity has made it a beacon of quality education in the region, shaping future generations of leaders and innovators.
