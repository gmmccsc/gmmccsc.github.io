Who we are...?

Inspired by Carl Sagan’s profound words, “Somewhere, something incredible is waiting to be known,” the Govt. M.M. City College Science Club was founded on _______, 2025 by Dr. Zahir Raihan, the department head of Physics at Govt. M.M. City College. Established with a vision to look beyond textbooks, our club serves as a launchpad for students eager to explore the vast, interconnected realms of modern science. Today, with a thriving network of over 100 dedicated members and accomplished alumni, we have established ourselves as a premier hub for scientific enthusiasm and academic excellence in the region.

We operate under the guiding principle that curiosity is the engine of human progress. The club provides a collaborative space where analytical thinking meets creative problem-solving, allowing members to bridge the gap between theoretical classroom concepts and real-world breakthroughs. Throughout the academic year, our campus buzzes with intellectual activity, including interactive weekly group discussions, specialized workshops, guest lectures by prominent scientists and academics and intra-college science olympiads.

Demystifying Science & Creative Expression

Recognizing that complex formulas and rigid academic pressure can sometimes alienate students, the club is deeply committed to making science approachable, engaging, and deeply rewarding. We firmly believe that communication is just as vital as calculation.[publication part]

The Govt. M.M. City College Science Club remains a sanctuary for thinkers, creators, and innovators. We warmly welcome students of all academic backgrounds and skill levels to join our ranks. Guided by experienced mentors and driven by an unstoppable spirit of inquiry, we strive to remain at the absolute forefront of scientific literacy—inspiring the next generation of pioneers to question the unknown and shape the future.

Membership

Welcome all science maniacs, to the world of numbers, logic and limitless curiosity! If you're a student who loves to explore patterns, solving problems and finds beauty in science, this is your page.

Who Can Join?

All students are welcomed to be a part of the Science Club. Whether you are a science prodigy or just a curious learner, your mind of interest is what matters the most!

How to Join?

Membership opens during your college 1st year. Simply fill out the Club Entrance Form when registration begins (Online or Offline).

Membership Process

  1. Collect the Membership Form from the designated point for Tk. 20.
  2. Submit the completed form within the announced deadline along with the Tk. 200 membership fee.

That's it, no additional fees for the rest of your college life! Within about three months, you will receive your official Club ID Card.

Communication & Community

After registration, all general members will be added to an official WhatsApp and/or Messenger group, where you will receive updates, event notices, and scientific inspiration throughout the year.

Group Representative (GR)

From your respective groups there will be one or two group representatives, selected by the Executive Members, who will lead their group. They will give announcements and notify all the general members about updates.

Explore & Engage

Once you're a member, you can choose to join different departments of the club - whether it's event organizing, research, publications or outreach. Each department offers a chance to develop new skills and contribute to the club's legacy.
